# Diagnostic template

## Record

Record symptoms and scope, candidate causes, evidence that distinguishes them, next actions, source/test links and known limitations.

## Do not record

Do not record raw incident logs, personal data, secrets, unverified root causes, or operational actions outside the caller's authorization.

## Ownership and verification

Name the owning module and link the applicable contract, source, tests and version. Mark unresolved claims and verification gaps.
